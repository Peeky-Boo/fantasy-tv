Fantasy TV

Pop-ups over RedZone (or anything on your Fire TV) when someone in your Sleeper league scores: touchdowns, sacks, big plays, lead changes, with a live scoreboard in the corner. Points come straight from Sleeper, so they match your league exactly.

1 Find your league

Your Sleeper username (Sleeper app: tap your avatar, then Settings).

2 Pick the look

Pop-ups in the top right
Sound

Real stadium sounds: an air horn and the crowd when you score, booing when your opponent does, a roar for your sacks and big plays, a groan when you turn it over, a ding when the lead changes. Turn it off if the picture stutters when one plays.
